<h3>Case Study Outline</h3>

<h3></h3>Business Problem:</h3>

The Retail Business wants to understand which products get the most orders and also drive the most revenue. The business also would like to know when customers are most active, in order to optimize product offerings, staffing, and sales strategy.

The reason for this is that despite having consistent operations, the business lacks clarity on which items contribute the most to revenue and the specific times customers are most likely to purchase. This limits its ability to optimize inventory, pricing, and staffing decisions.

<h3>Tools:</h3>
Excel, Power Query, and Pivot tables.
<h3>Features:</h3>

•	InvoiceNo

•	StockCode

•	Description

•	Quantity

•	UnitPrice

•	InvoiceDate

•	CustomerID

•	Country

DATA CLEANING PROCESS using Power Query:

•	Starting with Invoice ID, I cleaned and removed all invoice ID’s starting with the letter ‘c.’

•	Removed duplicates from the data.

•	Added a TotalSales column by multiplying Quantity by UnitPrice

•	Checked for duplicates and Null values

<h3>KEY FINDINGS:</h3>

<h4>Best Selling Product:</h4>
•	The Best-Selling Product By Quantity: The best-selling product by quantity bought so far was WORLD WAR 2 GLIDERS ASSTD DESIGNS, with an amount of 3744 quantities sold during the period of the analysis.

•	The Best-Selling Product By Invoice Count: WHITE HANGING HEART T-LIGHT HOLDER was the top product based on invoice count/orders

•	The Best Selling Product By Revenue: The best-selling product by Revenue is the REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER at  $7,880.40, generated despite not being the highest in units sold.

<h4>Sales By Hour:</h4>

•	Peak Purchasing Hour is at 16:00 hours with a revenue of $47,694, which is considered a prime time for finding discounted prices in the UK.

<h4>Sales By Day of the Week:</h4>

•	Customer purchases during the days of the week peak on Friday, with an amount of $65,974 in revenue for the period covered, suggesting possible weekend shopping.

<h4>Sales By Month:</h4>

•	During July, we see a peak in customer purchases, with $51,037 generated in revenue, possibly driven by warm weather conditions that favour interior designs and moving houses.

<h3>Recommendations</h3>

•	Prioritize consistent stocking of high-volume items like WORLD WAR 2 GLIDERS ASSTD DESIGNS in order not to hurt fulfillment rates.

•	Priorities should be made for marketing and increasing the visibility of top revenue products since they have the highest impact on revenue generation.

•	Launch promotions and increase stock during peak months to maximise seasonal surge.

•	Launch discount campaigns during less performing months to draw in customers.

•	Schedule sales campaigns targeting customers right before peak hours as a reminder to customers.
